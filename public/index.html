Questions

What data do I need?

Whatever you already keep: a till export, a spreadsheet, invoices or photos of receipts. If it records what was sold, when and for how much, it works.

Is my data safe?

Your data is used only to build your report. Customer names and phone numbers are never copied into it.

Why not just ask an AI chatbot?

You can, if you have the time to clean the data, check the numbers and research your market. We do that work and hand you a finished, checked report.
