Finding 01

Not all sales are equal

Some products leave far more at the booth than others for the same customer and the same effort. The Snapshot ranks every category by what it really earns, so staff know what to recommend first.

Margins and supplier prices stay private to the client and are not published here.

Finding 02

Four hours decide the day

316 of 652 sales happened between 11:00 and 15:00. That is when two people should be at the counter, and nobody on a break.

Finding 03

Same price as the big platforms

We compared eight products with GetYourGuide in Costa Adeje. Prices were the same. The booth wins on advice in person, paying only a deposit, and someone to call if plans change, but the platforms win on reviews. So reviews became part of the plan.

The plan

Five changes

01

Recommend first what pays most

Put the best-earning products first. Estimated +€1,400 per quarter if 10% of sales shift.

02

Same price, better service

A sign: same price as online, pay only the deposit, book on WhatsApp.

03

Language by season

Summer: Spanish visitors are 16% of the island but 8% of customers. Winter: German and Nordic.

04

Two people at peak

Full staff from 11:00 to 15:00.

05

Digital tickets

A 30-second form per sale replaces photos and makes next month's report automatic.
