You do not need a data team. You need answers.

Why most small businesses already own the data that would grow them, and what stops them using it.

Large companies employ analysts to read their numbers. Small businesses rarely can, so decisions are made on instinct: who to staff, what to recommend, which customers to chase.

The data to answer those questions is usually already there. It is just scattered and unread. One careful pass through it often finds a change worth more than the cost of looking.

That is the idea behind the Business Snapshot: one report, a fixed price, and a plan you can act on the next morning.
