652 handwritten tickets, one clear plan

What one summer of sales at an excursion booth in Tenerife revealed about hours, margins and customers.

Our first Snapshot was for a family-run excursion booth in the south of Tenerife. Its staff sent a photo of every handwritten ticket to a WhatsApp group: 652 sales from July to October 2026.

From those photos we found that 48% of sales happen between 11:00 and 15:00, that some products earn the booth far more than others, and that German and Spanish visitors are under-represented compared with the island as a whole.
