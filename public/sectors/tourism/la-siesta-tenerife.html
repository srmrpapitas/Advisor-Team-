Location

One street, many booths

La Siesta sits on the main walk above Playa de las Vistas, next to a massage shop, with several excursion booths on either side. They all sell the same trips from the same providers.

Competitors

Seasons

Winter and summer are two different businesses

The tickets cover one summer. Official arrival data shows who comes the rest of the year, and how old they are.

15×

Nordic visitors in winter

47,700 arrived in January 2025 against 3,200 in August. They come from November to March.

31%

Over 60 in winter

From January to March, almost a third of visitors are over 60. From April to June it falls to 26% and under-30s rise to 26%.

3×

Young people book more boats

18% of visitors under 25 take a boat trip, against 6% of those over 60. Older visitors prefer guided, organised trips.

Winter: sell comfort

Guided Teide tours, whale watching on large catamarans, coach trips and Loro Parque. Signs in German and Swedish or Norwegian.

Summer: sell adrenaline

Jet skis, parasailing, buggies, Siam Park and swim stops. Signs in Spanish and Italian, booking on WhatsApp.

All year: keep the regulars

Winter visitors stay about ten nights and often return each year. Keep their contact and offer the next trip.

Tickets per week, summer 2026. The five changes are estimated to raise what the booth earns by about 6%.

Weeks start on Monday; the first and last weeks are incomplete. The 6% is an estimate, not a guarantee. Earnings, margins and supplier prices stay private to the client.

The plan

Five changes

01

Recommend first what pays most

Some products leave far more at the booth than others. Staff now know which ones.

02

Same price, better service

A sign: same price as online, pay only the deposit, book on WhatsApp.

03

Language by season

Summer: Spanish visitors are 16% of the island but 8% of customers. Winter: German and Nordic.

04

Two people at peak

Full staff from 11:00 to 15:00.

05

Digital tickets

A 30-second form per sale makes next month's report automatic.
